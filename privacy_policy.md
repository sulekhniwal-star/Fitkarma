# FitKarma — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 31, 2026**

FitKarma ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy in full compliance with **India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** and global health data privacy standards.

## 1. Data Minimization & Collection
- We collect biometrics (height, weight, age), step counts, sleep logs, and nutrition records strictly to calculate your Health OS Daily Intelligence Package (DIP).
- All metabolic and readiness scores are computed locally on your device.

## 2. DPDP Act Rights & Data Erasure
- You have the right to request full erasure of your personal data at any time via Settings -> Account Deletion.
- Account deletion purges all local Drift database records, Sentry diagnostic logs, and Cloudflare AI cache entries associated with your account.

## 3. Medical Disclaimer
- FitKarma is a wellness and health tracking application. It is **not** a licensed medical diagnostic tool. Always consult a qualified physician before undertaking new intense exercise or diet programs.
